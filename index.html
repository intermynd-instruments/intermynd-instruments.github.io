/ Approach ■

Built around the musical loop.

DS-8 expresses the Intermynd approach: move from the first hit to a complete performance without losing momentum or changing tools.

Immediate control

Make and edit rhythms without interrupting playback. The controls stay close to the musical decision in front of you.

One continuous workflow

Synthesize, sample, sequence, arrange, and perform inside one instrument instead of stitching together disconnected tools.

Portable performance

Physical controls, projects, presets, and Live  FX keep the full creative and performance workflow inside the Vita.

/ Mission ■

From idea to performance, without leaving the instrument.

Intermynd builds focused musical tools that keep the important actions together. DS-8 starts with rhythm and carries it through sound design, sampling, arrangement, performance, and capture.
